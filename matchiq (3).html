How it works

Two tools. One engine.

For candidates Job seekers

1

Enter the role

Type in the job title and paste the full job description from any posting.

2

Upload your CV

Drop your CV as a PDF or paste your skills summary directly into the text area.

3

Get your match report

Receive a score, skill breakdown, and four concrete suggestions to strengthen your application.

4

Generate a cover letter

One click creates a tailored cover letter built from your analysis — ready to send.

For recruiters HR teams

1

Paste the job description

One job description is all you need. Paste it once and run it against multiple candidates.

2

Add your candidates

Add up to 10 candidates by name and CV. Mix and match — paste CVs or type summaries.

3

Get the ranked shortlist

A live progress bar runs each analysis. Results appear as a ranked leaderboard you can drill into.

4

Export and decide

Download the full ranking as a CSV to share with your team or integrate into your process.
